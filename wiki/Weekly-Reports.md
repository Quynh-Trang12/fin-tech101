# Weekly Reports

## Purpose

Task C.1 requires the project Wiki to hold all project documentation, including the weekly reports submitted for each task. This page indexes those reports so that progress across Tasks C.1–C.7 can be reviewed from a single location.

Each report documents the objective, implementation approach, and outcome of its corresponding task, together with supporting screenshots and results stored alongside it in the repository.
